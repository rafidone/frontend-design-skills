02 — Rules of the wood

The rules change when the weather does.

Survival comes from reading a place, not from filling a bar. The forest speaks in small signals if you stay long enough to notice.

Season Rule of the wood Field signal

Spring

Take only what returns.

Berries, bark and game follow the season. Overharvest a grove and it may not come back next spring.

Regrowth82%

Autumn

A shelter that shows its history.

Walls settle, roofs sag and repairs leave visible scars. A camp you abandon is still a place the forest can find.

Integrity64%

Winter

The Long Night rewards preparation.

Cold, hunger and old decisions arrive together. There is no timer to beat, only the store you laid in during autumn.

Stores41%
