One camp · One winter

Survive a forest with a memory.

Every camp leaves a mark. Cut too much and the canopy thins; ignore the old roads and something else starts using them. The world keeps score without a quest marker.

Read the field notes →
01 · Build A

A shelter that shows its history.

Walls settle, roofs sag and repairs leave visible scars. A camp you abandon is still a place the forest can find.

02 · Forage B

Take only what returns.

Berries, bark and game follow the seasons. Overharvest a grove and it may not come back next spring.

03 · Endure C

Winter changes the rules.

Cold, hunger and old decisions arrive together. The Long Night rewards preparation, not speed.

A world that answers back.
