01 — Construction

Nothing here is bonded for life.

Most keyboards are replaced when one part fails. Lattice starts from the opposite premise: keep the body, open the plate and change the parts that wear.

01 / Mass

Weight where it matters.

The case carries the mass so the board stays put. The internals stay light enough to lift out by hand.

02 / Feel

Flex, tuned by feel.

A 0.8 mm plate and a narrow gasket give a little give at the edges and a firm centre.

03 / Access

The back comes off.

Four screws expose the controller, cable and plate. A failed switch is a two-minute repair.

Can I change the switches later?

Yes. The plate is hot-swap and the case opens with four screws. The repair guide covers switch, plate and controller replacement.

What does the ten-year promise cover?

Parts and labour for anything that fails in normal use. Wear items such as keycaps and cables are sold at cost after the first two years.
