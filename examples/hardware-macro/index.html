01 / Construction

Open the case, keep the board.

Most keyboards are discarded when one switch fails. Lattice starts from the opposite assumption: the shell stays, the wearing parts come out.

See the repair sequence →

Opening time: 90 seconds, one hex key.

  1. 01 / Mass

    Mass where it counts.

    The case carries the weight so the board stays put. The internals stay light enough to lift out by hand.

  2. 02 / Feel

    Flex, measured by hand.

    A 0.8 mm plate on a narrow gasket gives at the edges and holds firm at the centre.

  3. 03 / Access

    The back comes off.

    Four screws expose the controller, cable and plate. A failed switch is a two-minute repair.

Can I change the switches later?

Yes. The plate is hot-swap and the case opens with four screws. The repair guide covers switch, plate and controller replacement.

What does the ten-year promise cover?

Parts and labour for anything that fails in normal use. Wear items such as keycaps and cables are sold at cost after the first two years.
