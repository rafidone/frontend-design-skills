A small menu, made properly.

Three things matter here: the coffee, the bread and the room. We keep the menu short so each one gets the attention it needs, and we stop when the bread runs out.

Read the menu →
01 · Coffee A

A short, seasonal list.

One espresso blend and one filter, both roasted in Lisbon. Oat and whole milk, no syrups, no size ladder.

02 · Bread B

Baked before dawn.

Country loaves, rye and pastries from a slow levain. The first bake lands at seven; the last is usually gone by two.

03 · Room C

A table for an hour.

Twenty-four seats, one shared table and a window bench. No laptops after noon on weekends, so the room stays a room.

A neighbourhood place, not a concept.

Open since 2019, on the same corner, with the same two ovens and a very short menu.

What changed

  • Bread is baked on site every morning.
  • The menu changes with the market, not the calendar.
  • The back table is kept for anyone who asks.

Do you take reservations?

Yes, for tables of four or more. The back table can be booked for two hours; everything else is walk-in.

Is there a vegan option?

The menu always has one vegan bake and oat milk for coffee. Ask at the counter and we will tell you what is in the case.
