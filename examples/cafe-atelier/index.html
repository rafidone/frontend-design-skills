Before the city wakes, the oven is already lit.

We are one coffee house on a Lisbon corner, keeping the same hours and the same two ovens. This is our record of the work — how it is done, what is in the cup, and when to come by.

01 — Method

The work, in four movements.

Nothing here is a concept. Bread is mixed at five, the filter is ground to order, and the room is opened by the same person who closes it.

The house method · a working record

01

Mix before dawn

Flour, water, salt and time. The country loaf is shaped by hand and proved slowly, because a rushed dough carries the hurry into the morning.

05:00
02

Grind to order

Beans are weighed for each cup, never left sitting in a hopper. The filter changes with the roast and with the weather outside the door.

07:00
03

Open the room

Windows at seven, the shared table wiped down twice, and the back table left unreserved for whoever asks for an hour with a book.

07:00
04

Keep the record

Every lot is noted on the board and in the ledger below. What we serve is what we can name, and what we cannot name we do not sell.

16:00

02 — Proof

What the cup tells us.

A ledger of what is on the bar this week, kept in the open. Origins, roast days and the notes we would want if we were sitting down to read them.

03 — The menu

A short card, on two pages.

Prices in euro. Oat milk instead of dairy, no syrups and no size ladder. Ask at the counter for whatever is still warm.
