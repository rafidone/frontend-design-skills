Inside the engagement

A review that ends with an answer.

Most teams do not need more feedback. They need a room where the right people can make one decision and let the work move. We redesign the forum around that single job.

  1. Arrive with the choice

    The maker names the fork in the road before the meeting starts. Context is sent ahead, not presented live.

  2. Discuss against the brief

    Feedback gets measured against the agreed problem, not personal preference. The loudest voice is not the default.

  3. Leave with an owner

    One decision, one owner, one date. It lands in the project record before anyone opens their laptop.
