Questions we hear before the first call.

Do we need to change our tools?

No. Morrow works with the tools you already pay for. The first job is to make the current setup legible; replacing software is a separate decision, and often unnecessary.

How much of the team needs to be involved?

A design lead, a producer and two or three makers is enough for the audit. For an embedded engagement, we ask for access to the full team’s week and a sponsor with authority to change it.

Is this coaching or consulting?

A little of both, in plain language. We diagnose the system, make changes alongside the team and leave the team able to keep them. We do not deliver a deck and disappear.

What if the problem is leadership, not process?

Then we say so early. Some engagements become a conversation about decision rights, capacity or priorities. We can facilitate that work, or help you name it clearly enough to take elsewhere.

Can you work across time zones?

Yes. We keep a four-hour overlap with your core team and put as much as possible in writing. The studio is based in Lisbon and works regularly with teams in London, Berlin and New York.

Still unsure?

Bring the messy version.

You do not need a tidy brief or a diagnosis. A sentence about the week that keeps going wrong is enough to start.
