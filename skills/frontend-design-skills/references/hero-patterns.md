# Hero Patterns Reference

A hero makes the offer, audience, and next action clear while providing the first
piece of evidence. Choose one archetype and obey its grid and copy limits. Do not
blend three archetypes into a collage.

## Chooser
| Archetype | Best fit | Evidence | Motion |
|-----------|----------|----------|--------|
| Product surface split | SaaS, devtool | Real UI | T1 |
| Copy-led split | Service, consulting | Person or result | T1 |
| Stage demo | AI, technical | Input/output | T1/T2 |
| Full-bleed place | Hospitality, gaming | Photograph or key art | T1/T2 |
| Object study | Hardware, commerce | Product photograph | T1 |
| Editorial masthead | Editorial, report | Lead story | T0 |
| Index hero | Commerce, archive | Catalog grid | T1 |
| Before/after | Service, manufacturing | Transformation | T1 |
| Data proof | B2B, research | Chart or benchmark | T0/T1 |
| Invitation | Hospitality, events | Form plus place | T1 |

---

## 1. Product surface split

**Structure.** 5 columns copy, 7 columns product UI; media starts at column 7 and
aligns with the second headline line.

**Copy.** Optional 2–4 word eyebrow; 3–10 word display; 12–24 word lead naming
audience and outcome; one primary and one quiet action; one 8–16 word trust line.

**Media.** Real screen at 1440-wide capture, cropped to the task, one window, one
visible state. Labels must be readable at rendered size; if they fall below 12px,
crop closer or rebuild the fragment.

**Rules.** No tilted perspective card, no gradient stage, no floating panels. Use
one border and one shadow. Phone: copy, 4:3 crop of the core surface, repeat
action.

**Motion.** T1 entrance; media after copy. No parallax.

**Fails.** Fake dashboard, unreadable labels, three windows, copy wider than 6
columns.

---

## 2. Copy-led split

**Structure.** 6 columns copy, 5 columns image, 1 column gap.

**Copy.** Promise plus place or audience; 12–24 word lead; call or book primary,
services secondary; proof line with license, years, response time, or result.

**Media.** Documentary portrait or real before/after at 4:5 or 3:4. No stock team
meeting. The subject may look into the copy to lead the eye to the action.

**Rules.** Image edge aligns to a grid column unless the section is full-bleed.
Phone: copy, image, repeated action. Do not ask for a long form before explaining
the service.

**Motion.** T1 or T0; one fade is enough.

**Fails.** Generic smiling team, no location, no price signal, a form that asks
too much before the promise.

---

## 3. Stage demo

**Structure.** Wide demo surface as the focal point, compact copy above or beside.

**Copy.** Task the system performs, not the technology category; input type,
output type, constraint; try demo and read docs; model, date, latency, or
environment.

**Media.** Real input/output pair or interactive demo with a static transcript.
Show the prompt, the result, and one failure or limit. Label simulated output as
a demonstration.

**Rules.** No unpausable autoplay. Keep the demo within 1H; provide "view full
example" instead of a pinned hero. Static transcript for crawlers, no-JS, and
reduced motion.

**Motion.** T1; T2 only when the demo is real and the fallback is complete.

**Fails.** Glowing orb, robot mascot, invented benchmark, hidden prompt, output
that does not match the claim.

---

## 4. Full-bleed place

**Structure.** One photograph or key art fills the first screen; copy in a
protected area with a scrim or solid field; booking or play action visible.

**Copy.** Name and place, or title and release window; one sentence ≤18 words; one
primary action plus key facts (dates, platforms, hours).

**Media.** Commissioned or licensed photography of the actual place, or key art
that represents the actual game. 16:9 desktop, 4:3 phone.

**Rules.** Text contrast 4.5:1 at the worst pixel. Do not put three text blocks in
three corners. Keep the action in the thumb zone. Provide a poster for video. No
five-image carousel.

**Motion.** T1 for photography; T2 for gaming with a static poster.

**Fails.** Generic resort photo, white text on a bright sky, autoplay sound, no
dates or location.

---

## 5. Object study

**Structure.** Large product photograph on a neutral field; compact copy column;
spec rail. The object occupies 55–65% of the width.

**Copy.** Product name plus defining property; one measured fact; price or "from";
primary action and spec link.

**Media.** Studio photograph with controlled shadow, one angle, and a scale cue.
A second macro detail belongs below the fold.

**Rules.** Disclose renders. Show real ports, controls, and materials. Keep the
background true to the product color. Phone: crop to the defining detail, not the
whole object at 20% width.

**Motion.** T1; T2 for a 3D reveal with poster and no-WebGL path.

**Fails.** Floating object with no scale, impossible reflections, specs without
units, a rotating model that blocks the CTA.

---

## 6. Editorial masthead

**Structure.** Publication name, issue or date, lead story at 8 columns, one
supporting story at 4; rules separate blocks.

**Copy.** Masthead; lead headline, deck, byline, date; one secondary headline and
a latest list.

**Media.** One lead image or commissioned illustration, plus small index images.
No hero animation.

**Rules.** Headline and deck match the article. Byline and date are visible.
Reading measure starts in the article, not the masthead. Lead image at 3:2 or
16:10; no portrait squeezed into a banner.

**Motion.** T0; link hover only.

**Fails.** No date or byline, an ad as the lead story, an oversized masthead that
pushes the story below the fold.

---

## 7. Index hero

**Structure.** Compact copy block plus a product grid that begins in the first
screen. The grid is the hero.

**Copy.** Category line; short display naming the collection; visible filter and
sort; result count and active filters.

**Media.** Product cutouts on one ground, same ratio and shadow. Show one full card
on phone and at least one row on desktop.

**Rules.** Price and variant count on the card. No campaign image hiding the
catalog. Filters usable at 320px. Sort state announced.

**Motion.** T1; image swap only when a second image exists; no autoplay.

**Fails.** Hero pushes products below two screens, mixed crops, hidden prices,
filters that do not update the count.

---

## 8. Before/after

**Structure.** Two matched images with the same crop and lighting, divider or
labeled pair; copy above or beside, never over the comparison.

**Copy.** Transformation in one line; method or timeframe in one line; one action.

**Media.** Real matched pairs from the same position, lens, and light.

**Rules.** Label "Before" and "After" in text. Keyboard-operable slider. Static
side-by-side fallback. No misleading lighting or retouching.

**Motion.** T1; slider follows the user, no autoplay wipe.

**Fails.** Different crops, misleading light, unlabeled images, a slider that
traps focus.

---

## 9. Data proof

**Structure.** Claim at 5 columns, chart at 7. The chart is the evidence.

**Copy.** Plain claim; method and date; read the report or view the data.

**Media.** Chart from a real dataset, or benchmark with documented method. Title,
units, axis labels, source. Show the baseline; do not truncate a bar axis.

**Rules.** Provide a data table or text summary. Cite source and date. No 3D
charts, gradient fills, or animated counters that hide the final value.

**Motion.** T0/T1; count-up only if the final value is in the HTML.

**Fails.** No axis, no source, cherry-picked range, invented data, unreadable
mobile labels.

---

## 10. Invitation

**Structure.** Place or event image plus a compact booking or registration form in
the first screen. The form is the action.

**Copy.** Name, place, date or hours; one sentence of purpose; 2–3 fields; policy
or price line.

**Media.** Real place photograph, not a texture. Phone: image band above the form,
not a full-screen background behind inputs.

**Rules.** Visible 16px labels. First step works without JS. Inline errors. Do not
ask for a phone number unless the business calls. Show the next step and
cancellation policy before submission.

**Motion.** T1; no entrance that delays the form.

**Fails.** Form over a busy image, unlabeled fields, a date picker that cannot be
typed, hidden fees, no confirmation.

---

## Shared checks
| Check | Pass condition |
|-------|----------------|
| Focus and specificity | The eye lands on evidence; a competitor cannot swap its logo unchanged |
| Copy and action | Display ≤10 words; lead ≤24; one primary action visible |
| Proof and grid | One verifiable element; copy and media align to documented columns |
| Phone, contrast, motion | Fits 1H plus 15%; 4.5:1 at worst pixel; readable before entrance completes |

## Anti-patterns

Eyebrow/pill/kicker stacks; three equal CTAs; unrelated hero image; meaningless
gradient; text over photo without a measured scrim; unpausable carousel;
full-screen video hiding the action; "scroll to explore" when the next section is
visible; invented first-screen metric; a phone hero two screens tall.
